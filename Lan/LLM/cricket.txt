Cricket is one of the most popular sports in the world, especially in countries like India, Australia, England, and Pakistan. It is played between two teams of 11 players each and combines strategy, skill, endurance, and teamwork.

🏟 Basic Objective

One team bats and tries to score runs.

The other team bowls and fields to restrict runs and take wickets.

Teams switch roles after an innings.

The team with more runs wins.

📜 Formats of Cricket

Cricket has three major international formats:

1️⃣ Test Cricket

Longest format (up to 5 days)

Considered the purest form

Played in white clothing with a red ball

2️⃣ One Day Internationals (ODIs)

50 overs per side

Completed in one day

Played in colored jerseys

3️⃣ T20 (Twenty20)

20 overs per side

Fast-paced and aggressive

Very popular globally

The biggest T20 league in the world is the Indian Premier League.

🌍 Governing Body

International cricket is governed by the
International Cricket Council (ICC).

Major ICC tournaments include:

ICC Cricket World Cup (ODI)

ICC T20 World Cup

ICC World Test Championship

🧠 Why Cricket Is Unique

Highly strategic field placements

Different pitch conditions affect gameplay

Weather and swing play a role

Balance between batting, bowling, and fielding

🔥 Fun Fact

Cricket originated in England in the 16th century and later spread across the world through British influence.
